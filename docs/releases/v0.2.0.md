**FF7Recomp for iOS 0.2.0: pre-release.** Mods: get them from 7th Heaven's catalogs in the app and they load in the game. The game now pauses cleanly when you leave the app or take a call. Still early: expect rough edges.

> **No game is included.** You need your own Steam copy of FINAL FANTASY VII. The builder carries the engine but no game code and no game files. The IPA it makes holds the game built from your copy and your game files: **for your own devices only, never share it.** Not affiliated with or endorsed by Square Enix.

## Download

`FF7Recomp-Builder-0.2.0.zip`: unzip it and move **FF7Recomp Builder** to Applications. Build a new FF7Recomp.ipa with it and install it over 0.1.0; your saves, save states and mods stay.

## What's new

- **Get Mods** (Mods → Get Mods): browse 7th Heaven's Qhimm and Sega catalogs, search them, filter by category, see each mod's preview, description and size, and install or update it. Downloads run one at a time while you browse, and a download that's cut off resumes later. `.iro`, `.zip` and `.7z` mods install, Google Drive links included; mods shared on Mega or forum pages open in Safari to download by hand.
- **Mods load in the game**, in the order of your Mods list (higher wins when two mods replace the same file). A new mod goes to the bottom of the list, switched on; an update keeps its place.
- **Conflict warnings:** before installing, when two mods likely do the same thing (two gameplay overhauls, two soundtracks); after, the game files two enabled mods both replace, and mods that say they don't work together.
- **Leaving the app or a call** pauses the game and its music and sound; it goes on where it was. Before, a call could leave the music silent.
- **The silent switch** now mutes the game.

## What you need

- A Mac with **Apple silicon** (M1 or later). Intel Macs are not supported yet.
- **Xcode**, installed from the App Store and opened once.
- **FINAL FANTASY VII from Steam**, as Steam installs it today (`FINAL FANTASY VII Steam Edition`). No patch or older version is needed.
- A way to sign and install an IPA on your iPhone: a development profile, AltStore, SideStore or similar. Keep the **extended virtual addressing** entitlement if your profile has it.
- About 7 GB free on the Mac while it builds; the IPA is about 3 GB. Mods need room on the iPhone too: a download needs about twice its size free while it unpacks.

## How to use it

1. Open FF7Recomp Builder. The app is not notarized, so the first time macOS says it can't check it: right-click it → **Open**, or go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → **Open Anyway**.
2. It finds your Steam copy (or choose the folder) and checks Xcode is there.
3. **Build FF7Recomp.ipa…**, then sign the IPA and install it. Open FF7Recomp: it confirms your game files, and **Play** starts the game.

## Known gaps

- Mods are tested in the simulator (a folder mod and a `.7z` catalog mod); `.iro` archives and large texture packs on an iPhone are next. Mod options (7th Heaven's configuration screen) can't be set yet, and RAR archives aren't supported.
- **Quitting** (✕ → Quit) closes the app; open it again to play.
- Tested on an iPhone Duo and in the simulator only; other iPhones and iPads are untested.
