**FF7Recomp for iOS 0.1.0: first pre-release.** FF7Recomp Builder, a Mac app, builds FINAL FANTASY VII for your iPhone from your own Steam copy. It is early: expect rough edges.

> **No game is included.** You need your own Steam copy of FINAL FANTASY VII. The builder carries the engine but no game code and no game files. The IPA it makes holds the game built from your copy and your game files: **for your own devices only, never share it.** Not affiliated with or endorsed by Square Enix.

## Download

`FF7Recomp-Builder-0.1.0.zip`: unzip it and move **FF7Recomp Builder** to Applications.

## What you need

- A Mac with **Apple silicon** (M1 or later). Intel Macs are not supported yet.
- **Xcode**, installed from the App Store and opened once. Building for iPhone needs its iOS SDK and compiler.
- **FINAL FANTASY VII from Steam**, as Steam installs it today (`FINAL FANTASY VII Steam Edition`, the one with `ff7` inside). No patch or older version is needed. Patched game files are not supported.
- A way to sign and install an IPA on your iPhone: a development profile, AltStore, SideStore or similar. Keep the **extended virtual addressing** entitlement if your profile has it: the game reserves 4 GB of address space.
- About 7 GB free on the Mac while it builds; the IPA is about 3 GB.

## How to use it

1. Open FF7Recomp Builder. The app is not notarized, so the first time macOS says it can't check it: right-click it → **Open**, or go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → **Open Anyway**.
2. It finds your Steam copy (or choose the folder) and checks it is the 1.02 US build, and checks Xcode is there.
3. **Build FF7Recomp.ipa…** It lifts the game to C, compiles it for iPhone, links it with the engine and adds your game files. The first build takes a few minutes; later ones about a minute.
4. Sign the IPA and install it. Open FF7Recomp: it confirms your game files, and **Play** starts the game.

## What works

- The game from the intro through the first reactor and its boss, played on an iPhone Duo: field, battles, menus, movies, music and sound.
- **Touch pad:** a sliding 8-way d-pad, any number of fingers at once, every tap reaches the game however fast. It hides while a game controller is connected (Settings → Controls).
- **Game controllers** over Bluetooth.
- **iPhone Duo:** open or half-folded and upright, the game sits in the top half and a status panel in the bottom half (party HP/MP/Limit, location, time, gil), with quick buttons: Menu, save and load state, speed 1×/2×/3×, random battles on/off, Battle+. Closed, it plays on the outer screen.
- **Your files** in the Files app, under On My iPhone › FF7Recomp: Saves, Save States and Mods.

## Known gaps

- **Mods:** the Mods screen lists and orders mods, but applying them in game and downloading them in the app are not done yet.
- **Quitting** (✕ → Quit) closes the app; open it again to play.
- Tested on an iPhone Duo and in the simulator only; other iPhones and iPads are untested.
