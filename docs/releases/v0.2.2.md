**FF7Recomp for iOS 0.2.2: pre-release.** The frame rate setting works, up to 120 FPS.

> **No game is included.** You need your own Steam copy of FINAL FANTASY VII. The builder carries the engine but no game code and no game files. The IPA it makes holds the game built from your copy and your game files: **for your own devices only, never share it.** Not affiliated with or endorsed by Square Enix.

## Download

`FF7Recomp-Builder-0.2.2.zip`: unzip it and move **FF7Recomp Builder** to Applications. Build a new FF7Recomp.ipa with it and install it over 0.2.1; your saves, save states and mods stay.

## What's new

- **Frame rate** (Settings › Display): the setting was saved but never used; it now sets how fast the game runs, with the same choices as the Android port:
  - **Original:** as shipped (30 FPS in the field, 15 in battle, 60 in menus), timing bugs included.
  - **Original, fixed** (the default, as before): as shipped, with FFNx's fixes for the game's timing bugs.
  - **30 FPS battles:** battles at 30 instead of 15.
  - **60 FPS:** everything at 60.
  - **120 FPS (experimental):** the field at 120 on a 120 Hz screen, world map and battles at 60.

  It applies the next time the game starts. Tested on an iPhone Duo: every mode runs, and 120 FPS makes the field noticeably smoother. Higher rates use more battery.

## Known gaps

- Mods are tested in the simulator; `.iro` archives and large texture packs on an iPhone are next. RAR archives aren't supported.
- **Quitting** (✕ → Quit) closes the app; open it again to play.
- Tested on an iPhone Duo and in the simulator only.
