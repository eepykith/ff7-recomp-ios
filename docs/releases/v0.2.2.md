**FF7Recomp for iOS 0.2.2: pre-release.** Fixes from a longer playthrough on an iPhone Duo with eight mods (Echo-S, Enhanced Stock UI, Cosmo Memory, SYW Unified Field and Battle Textures and more).

> **No game is included.** You need your own Steam copy of FINAL FANTASY VII. The builder carries the engine but no game code and no game files. The IPA it makes holds the game built from your copy and your game files: **for your own devices only, never share it.** Not affiliated with or endorsed by Square Enix.

## Download

`FF7Recomp-Builder-0.2.2.zip`: unzip it and move **FF7Recomp Builder** to Applications. Build a new FF7Recomp.ipa with it and install it over 0.2.1; your saves, save states and mods stay.

## Fixed

- **Black field backgrounds after loading a save state** with texture mods (SYW Unified Field Textures and others). Loading a state now brings the mods' backgrounds back.
- **Quit** (✕ → Quit) no longer ends in a crash as the app closes: the game stops cleanly first.

## New

- **Engine log:** the game's own log (mod loading, sound, crash reports) is kept on the phone as `engine.log`, with the previous session's as `engine.prev.log`, in the app's private folder, so problems can be looked into after the fact.

## Known gaps

- Mods on Mega or forum pages open in Safari to download by hand; RAR archives aren't supported.
- **Quitting** closes the app; open it again to play.
- Tested on an iPhone Duo and in the simulator only.

See the [0.2.0 notes](https://github.com/eepykith/ff7-recomp-ios/releases/tag/v0.2.0) for what you need and how to use the builder. FF7Recomp for iOS is GPL-3.0.
