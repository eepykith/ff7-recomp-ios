**FF7Recomp for iOS 0.2.1: pre-release.** Fixes and additions to mods from testing 0.2.0 on an iPhone Duo.

> **No game is included.** You need your own Steam copy of FINAL FANTASY VII. The builder carries the engine but no game code and no game files. The IPA it makes holds the game built from your copy and your game files: **for your own devices only, never share it.** Not affiliated with or endorsed by Square Enix.

## Download

`FF7Recomp-Builder-0.2.1.zip`: unzip it and move **FF7Recomp Builder** to Applications. Build a new FF7Recomp.ipa with it and install it over 0.2.0; your saves, save states and mods stay.

## What's new

- **Mod options:** touch and hold a mod in the Mods list → **Options…**: its settings from its mod.xml (switches and choices, with descriptions), for folder and `.iro` mods. The game applies them the next time it starts.
- **Deleting a mod** (touch and hold → Delete, or swipe) asks first, and Get Mods no longer shows a deleted mod as installed, so you can download it again. A mod deleted in the Files app is noticed too.
- `.iro` mods now show their name, author and version in the Mods list.

## Known gaps

- Mods are tested in the simulator; `.iro` archives and large texture packs on an iPhone are next. RAR archives aren't supported.
- **Quitting** (✕ → Quit) closes the app; open it again to play.
- Tested on an iPhone Duo and in the simulator only.

See the [0.2.0 notes](https://github.com/eepykith/ff7-recomp-ios/releases/tag/v0.2.0) for what you need and how to use the builder. FF7Recomp for iOS is GPL-3.0.
