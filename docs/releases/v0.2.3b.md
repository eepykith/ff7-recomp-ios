**FF7Recomp for iOS 0.2.3b: pre-release.** A fix for the game not resuming after you lock the phone.

> **No game is included.** You need your own Steam copy of FINAL FANTASY VII. The builder carries the engine but no game code and no game files. The IPA it makes holds the game built from your copy and your game files: **for your own devices only, never share it.** Not affiliated with or endorsed by Square Enix.

## Download

`FF7Recomp-Builder-0.2.3b.zip`: unzip it and move **FF7Recomp Builder** to Applications. Build a new FF7Recomp.ipa with it and install it over 0.2.3a; your saves, save states and mods stay. (Inside, the apps report version 0.2.3, build 3: iOS version numbers can't have letters.)

## Fixed

- **The game stayed paused after locking and unlocking the phone** (or leaving the app for a while): the picture froze and only ✕ and Quit worked. iOS ends a suspended app's sound with an interruption it never says is over; FF7Recomp now takes its sound back when you return, and the game goes on. During a real phone call the game still waits until the call ends.

## Known gaps

- Mods on Mega or forum pages open in Safari to download by hand; RAR archives aren't supported.
- **Quitting** closes the app; open it again to play.
- Tested on an iPhone Duo and in the simulator only.

See the [0.2.0 notes](https://github.com/eepykith/ff7-recomp-ios/releases/tag/v0.2.0) for what you need and how to use the builder. FF7Recomp for iOS is GPL-3.0.
