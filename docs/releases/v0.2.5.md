**FF7Recomp for iOS 0.2.5: pre-release.** A guide reader in the game: find a guide on GameFAQs, save it, and read it beside the game on the iPhone Duo.

> **No game is included.** You need your own Steam copy of FINAL FANTASY VII. The builder carries the engine but no game code and no game files. The IPA it makes holds the game built from your copy and your game files: **for your own devices only, never share it.** Not affiliated with or endorsed by Square Enix.

## Download

`FF7Recomp-Builder-0.2.5.zip`: unzip it and move **FF7Recomp Builder** to Applications. Build a new FF7Recomp.ipa with it and install it over 0.2.4; your saves, save states and mods stay. The apps report version 0.2.5, build 1.

## New

- **📖 Guide**, next to ✕ while you play.
  - **iPhone Duo upright:** the guide takes the status panel's place and the game keeps running, so you can read and scroll while you play. The pad stays at the sides. Tap 📖 again for the status panel.
  - **Landscape, the Duo closed, or a regular iPhone:** the guide opens full screen and the game pauses until you close it.
- **Find guides on GameFAQs** opens GameFAQs' Final Fantasy VII guide list in the app. On a guide, **Save guide** keeps it for offline reading:
  - text guides are saved whole;
  - HTML guides split over many pages are joined into one file, and their table of contents jumps to each section.
- **Add a guide from Files…**, or put your own TXT, HTML or PDF guides in Files › On My iPhone › FF7Recomp › Guides.
- The reader keeps your place in each guide, and has find, text size, and a menu to switch guides. Text guides are sized so 80-column ASCII tables line up.

## If the FF7Recomp folder is missing from Files

Some signing apps drop the app's Files sharing when they re-sign. Re-sign with **file sharing / document browser** support turned on and install over the top; your files stay.

## Known gaps

- GameFAQs shows its own ads and privacy banner in the in-app browser.
- Links in a guide to other GameFAQs pages (item or enemy pages) open in Safari.
- A guide saved with 0.2.5's first test builds holds only its first page: delete it and save it again.
- Mods on Mega or forum pages open in Safari to download by hand; RAR archives aren't supported.
- **Quitting** closes the app; open it again to play.
- Tested on an iPhone Duo and in the simulator only.
