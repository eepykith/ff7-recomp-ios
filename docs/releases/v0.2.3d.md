**FF7Recomp for iOS 0.2.3d: pre-release.** A crash fix for save states, and the game picks up again when another app's audio stops.

> **No game is included.** You need your own Steam copy of FINAL FANTASY VII. The builder carries the engine but no game code and no game files. The IPA it makes holds the game built from your copy and your game files: **for your own devices only, never share it.** Not affiliated with or endorsed by Square Enix.

## Download

`FF7Recomp-Builder-0.2.3d.zip`: unzip it and move **FF7Recomp Builder** to Applications. Build a new FF7Recomp.ipa with it and install it over 0.2.3c; your saves, save states and mods stay. (Inside, the apps report version 0.2.3, build 5: iOS version numbers can't have letters.)

## Fixed

- **A crash in the field some time after loading a save state** (when a character's face was drawn). Another of FFNx's buffers kept its place from before the load; every such buffer now starts afresh after a load.
- **The game stayed paused after a picture-in-picture video (or music from another app) stopped**, until you brought FF7Recomp back from the app switcher. iOS doesn't say when another app's sound stops, so FF7Recomp now checks and goes on by itself within a second. During a phone call it still waits for the call to end.

## Known gaps

- Mods on Mega or forum pages open in Safari to download by hand; RAR archives aren't supported.
- **Quitting** closes the app; open it again to play.
- Tested on an iPhone Duo and in the simulator only.
