**FF7Recomp for iOS 0.2.4: pre-release.** A new on-screen pad you can arrange yourself, an easy way to send us your logs, simpler mod ordering, and a dark launcher.

> **No game is included.** You need your own Steam copy of FINAL FANTASY VII. The builder carries the engine but no game code and no game files. The IPA it makes holds the game built from your copy and your game files: **for your own devices only, never share it.** Not affiliated with or endorsed by Square Enix.

## Download

`FF7Recomp-Builder-0.2.4.zip`: unzip it and move **FF7Recomp Builder** to Applications. Build a new FF7Recomp.ipa with it and install it over 0.2.3d; your saves, save states and mods stay. The apps report version 0.2.4, build 1.

## New

- **A new on-screen pad.** See-through outlined buttons over the game: a round d-pad and big **OK** (B) and **Back** (A), with **Menu** (Y) and **Switch** (X) above them, each labelled with what it does. Sliding from button to button without lifting still works.
- **Buttons shown** (Settings › Controls): turn any of the d-pad, OK, Back, Menu, Switch, L1, R1, Start and Select on or off, or Show all. A hidden button still works on a game controller.
- **Size and layout** (Settings › Controls): drag any button to move it, tap one and use the slider to resize it, or resize the whole pad at once. Each way you hold the iPhone keeps its own layout (on the iPhone Duo: the outer screen, open in landscape, and upright). Play once on a screen before editing it so the editor matches the game exactly.
- **iPhone Duo upright:** the d-pad and face buttons sit high in the side columns, where your thumbs reach with your hands on the lower edge.
- **Share logs** (Settings › Help): one zip with the game's logs, your iPhone model and iOS version, and your mods in load order, ready to attach to a report. Nothing is sent unless you share it. The logs are also in Files › On My iPhone › FF7Recomp › Logs.
- **Report a problem** opens a new GitHub issue with your app version and device filled in.
- **After a crash**, the next launch offers to share the logs (once per crash).
- **Mod order:** the Mods list shows each mod's place in the load order (the first wins). On iOS 27 drag a mod to move it; touch and hold for Move to top, Move up and Move down.
- **Appearance** (Settings): the launcher follows your iPhone's light or dark setting, or stays light or dark. The game itself is unchanged.

## Known gaps

- Mods on Mega or forum pages open in Safari to download by hand; RAR archives aren't supported.
- **Quitting** closes the app; open it again to play.
- Tested on an iPhone Duo and in the simulator only.
