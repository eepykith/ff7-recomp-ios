**FF7Recomp for iOS 0.3a: pre-release.** A fix for Settings › Size and layout on the iPhone Duo's outer screen.

> **No game is included.** You need your own Steam copy of FINAL FANTASY VII. The builder carries the engine but no game code and no game files. The IPA it makes holds the game built from your copy and your game files: **for your own devices only, never share it.** Not affiliated with or endorsed by Square Enix.

## Download

`FF7Recomp-Builder-0.3a.zip`: unzip it and move **FF7Recomp Builder** to Applications. Build a new FF7Recomp.ipa with it and install it over 0.3; your saves, save states, mods, guides and pad layout stay. (Inside, the apps report version 0.3, build 2: iOS version numbers can't have letters.)

## Fixed

- **Size and layout on the iPhone Duo closed and held upright** showed the upright layout with the status panel, which the game never uses there, so changes made there had no effect. The editor now turns the way the game does: landscape on the outer screen, and upright with the status panel only while the Duo is open. Its hint to turn the iPhone shows only where turning changes the layout.

See the [0.3 notes](https://github.com/eepykith/ff7-recomp-ios/releases/tag/v0.3) for what's new in 0.3: widescreen, Download from Steam in the Builder, and Intel Macs.

## Known gaps

- Intel Macs: the Builder was tested under Rosetta on an Apple silicon Mac, not yet on an Intel Mac itself.
- A few full-screen pictures and movies stay 4:3 with bars at the sides in widescreen.
- GameFAQs shows its own ads and privacy banner in the in-app browser.
- Mods on Mega or forum pages open in Safari to download by hand; RAR archives aren't supported.
- **Quitting** closes the app; open it again to play.
- Tested on an iPhone Duo and in the simulator only.

See the [0.2.0 notes](https://github.com/eepykith/ff7-recomp-ios/releases/tag/v0.2.0) for what you need and how to use the builder. FF7Recomp for iOS is GPL-3.0.
