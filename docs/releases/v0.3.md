**FF7Recomp for iOS 0.3: pre-release.** Widescreen, the game files straight from Steam in the Builder, and the Builder now runs on Intel Macs too.

> **No game is included.** You need your own Steam copy of FINAL FANTASY VII. The builder carries the engine but no game code and no game files. The IPA it makes holds the game built from your copy and your game files: **for your own devices only, never share it.** Not affiliated with or endorsed by Square Enix.

## Download

`FF7Recomp-Builder-0.3.zip`: unzip it and move **FF7Recomp Builder** to Applications. Build a new FF7Recomp.ipa with it and install it over 0.2.5; your saves, save states, mods, guides and pad layout stay. The apps report version 0.3, build 1.

## New

- **Widescreen.** Settings › Picture: **16:9 Widescreen** (the new default) or **4:3 Original**. The field, world map and battles fill the wider screen; menus stay 4:3 in the middle. The choice takes effect the next time you press Play.
- **Download from Steam** in the Builder, for when the game isn't installed on your Mac:
  - sign in once by scanning a QR code with the Steam app on your phone (Steam Guard › Scan a QR code). You never type your Steam password into FF7Recomp; the sign-in is kept in your Mac's Keychain;
  - only the files the build needs come down (about 3.6 GB), each checked against Steam's checksum. If it stops, press Download again and it goes on where it was;
  - the files are kept in the Builder's own folder for your next build. **Remove…** deletes them when you want the space back; your own Steam install is never touched.
- **Intel Macs.** The Builder is now a universal app: Apple silicon or Intel, macOS 14 or later.

## Fixed

- With 16:9 on, the game no longer crashes at the start of a battle.

## Under the hood

- The engine core folder and scripts lost their old Android names (`external/ff7-recomp-core`, `tools/fetch_core.sh`, `tools/setup_core.sh`). If you build from source, run `tools/fetch_core.sh` again or rename your existing `external/ff7-recomp-android` folder.
- The Builder's Steam download is our own Swift code, after SteamKit2 and JavaSteam; it uses the LZMA SDK and zstd for Steam's compressed chunks.

## Known gaps

- Intel Macs: the Builder was tested under Rosetta on an Apple silicon Mac, not yet on an Intel Mac itself.
- A few full-screen pictures and movies stay 4:3 with bars at the sides in widescreen.
- On the iPhone Duo closed and held upright, Settings › Size and layout shows the upright layout, which the game doesn't use there; edit the layout with the phone turned landscape.
- GameFAQs shows its own ads and privacy banner in the in-app browser.
- Mods on Mega or forum pages open in Safari to download by hand; RAR archives aren't supported.
- **Quitting** closes the app; open it again to play.
- Tested on an iPhone Duo and in the simulator only.
